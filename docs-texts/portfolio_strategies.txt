This module implements the part 5 portfolio selection logic.

Main functions:
- project_to_simplex
- evaluate_portfolio
- refine_long_only_weights
- build_equal_weight_portfolio
- analyze_key_portfolios

Inputs:
- simulation dictionary from portfolio_simulation.py
- frontier dictionary from efficient_frontier.py

Outputs:
- dictionaries for Tangency, Min Variance, Max Sortino and Equal Weight portfolios
- optional visualization of optimal portfolios on the risk-return chart

Dependencies:
- common_utils.py
- project_config.py

Example:
- key_portfolios = analyze_key_portfolios(simulation, frontier)
